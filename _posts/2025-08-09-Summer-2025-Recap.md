We had a busy summer in the Cornett Lab! First up, we celebrated Christine, Jocelyne, and Malini at the IUSM graduation ceremony. We were also thrilled to welcome Morgan back to the lab. Morgan previously spent a summer working with us as an undergraduate, and she returned this summer as an MD student in the Indiana University Medical Student Program for Research and Scholarship (IMPRS). Alice also joined us through the Summer Undergraduate Research In Biomedical Science (SUREBS) program. They both gave excellent poster presentations at the end of the summer, and they will be missed! Finally, Taylor took home the top prize at the annual dessert contest that included some tasty and creative submissions!
